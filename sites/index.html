Geomorphic features

Lagoons

Earth’s coastal lagoons, with mapped boundaries, dimensions, and projected inundation by 2100 under two IPCC climate scenarios. The current dataset covers Greece.

View the website

Beachrocks

A global inventory of beachrocks, documenting coastal setting, cement mineralogy, formation processes, dating methods, and published sources.

View the website

Tombolos

Earth’s tombolos, with mapped dimensions, coastal connection types, and comparisons of current submergence with projected inundation by 2100. The current dataset covers Greece.

View the website

Natural disasters

Floods

Historic flood locations in Greece (1886–2022) from the NKUA catalogue, mainly riverine floods. Explore dates, impacts and supporting sources.

View the website

Landslides

Global and Greek landslide inventories, with mapped footprints, reported locations, original sources, and explicit spatial and temporal uncertainty.

View the website

Tsunamis

Explore tsunami sources, coastal observations and documented impacts through maps, filters, search and statistics.

View the website

Wildfires

Explore EFFIS and MTBS burned-area boundaries, wildfires and prescribed burns with source evidence, mapped extent and statistics.

View the website

Earthquakes

Explore significant earthquakes, documented historical impacts and instrumental event records from NOAA and USGS.

View the website

2026 Nepal Cascade Hazard Resources

A living catalogue of satellite data, hazard assessments, and humanitarian reports, with linked assets and documented search methods.

View the website

2026 Nepal Cascade Hazard

Interactive 2D and 3D reconstruction of the Bhote Koshi–Trishuli hazard cascade, integrating satellite imagery, terrain, flood extents, and documented impacts.

View the website

Water resources

Cyprus Irrigation Water Use

Net irrigation water consumption across Cyprus (2018/19–2025/26) from FAO WaPOR satellite evapotranspiration and a rainfall-only root-zone water balance, with uncertainty and priority zones.
