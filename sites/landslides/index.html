Catalogue coverage

Point records and footprints are source representations, not a deduplicated count of landslides or disasters. Overlapping inventories are retained with their provenance. A peer-reviewed dataset does not imply independent verification of every entry.

Coverage cells show record concentrations. A footprint pin is an interior display anchor; open the record to see its full outline. A point may be approximate. Missing dates and survey intervals remain distinct from known event dates.

Submit landslide data

Contribute a documented landslide, an inventory, or a correction. Use one row per source record and attach the original evidence and geometry where available.

Prepare your submission

Use the Data sheet and Field guide in the workbook, or the CSV with the same columns. Leave unknown values blank. Attach footprints as GeoJSON, GeoPackage or another documented GIS format with its coordinate reference system.

FieldWhat to provide

Send your contribution

  • Include contributor names, affiliation and contact details in your email.

What is a landslide?

A landslide occurs when gravity carries rock, debris or soil down a slope. The material may fall, topple, slide, spread or flow.

Definition source: USGS — What is a landslide and what causes one?.
